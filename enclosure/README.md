# Enclosure

3D-printable case for the player.

The source project is on
[OnShape](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/09c17f091a5bb896dc03a232/w/250e5cd355d39c508c5c4031/e/4855dac5da148417d96a0f04).

## Files

- `top_case`: top half of the case.
- `bottom_case`: bottom half of the case.
- `logos`: logo inserts.

Each part comes as `.step` (for editing or re-meshing) and `.stl` (medium quality).

## Hardware

The bottom case needs 8 threaded inserts for M3 screws (4 to hold the PCB and 4 to hold the top case). The holes have
been dimensioned for
[CNC Kitchen's threaded inserts](https://cnckitchen.store/products/heat-set-insert-m3-x-5-7-100-pieces).

8 M3 screws of length 8-10mm (not critical, can be a bit shorter or longer) are also required.

I used [this encoder knob](https://www.amazon.de/-/en/dp/B097PQGGCF) for a 6mm diameter shaft. The outer diameter is
32mm, the height is 13mm and the shaft hole is 10.5mm deep.

To act as light pipe (to guide the LED light to the top of the enclosure), I used 1.75mm clear PEGT filament segments.
They slide into the 18 holes and can be snipped flush with side cutters. I glued them with CA just to be safe but they
don't move too much on their own.
